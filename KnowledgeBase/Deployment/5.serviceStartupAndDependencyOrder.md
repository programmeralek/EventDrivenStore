## 5. Service Startup and Dependency Order

Although services do not strictly depend on startup order, a recommended sequence is:

1. Kafka and Zookeeper
2. Databases
3. Eureka Server
4. Gateway
5. Domain services (any order)

Services are designed to:
- Retry connections to Kafka
- Re-register with Eureka on restart
- Resume event consumption safely using idempotency

This allows the system to tolerate partial availability during startup or recovery.
